My Story

Beyond the Craft

I design digital experiences that people actually enjoy using. Blending technology, psychology, and strategy, I turn complex ideas into products that feel simple, intuitive, and human intractive with migiacl touch .

My story started with a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering. where I learned how systems think before diving into how people think. Along the way, I became fascinated by the gap between human behavior and technology—and that's where design found me.

Today, I blend UX, psychology, and data insights to create experiences that feel intuitive, reduce friction, and help users move from confusion to confidence. I see every product as a story, every interaction as a chapter, and every user as the main character.

Behavioral Observation

I closely observe how small, daily actions reflect user habits. Identifying unseen frictions—both offline and online—is where true product empathy begins.

Systems & Data Thinking

My engineering background helps me collaborate with development teams. I understand API constraints, data structures, and how backends serve frontend states.

Business & Speed Value

Reducing steps enhances user efficiency, which drives conversion rates. I design with measurable outcomes in mind—like task-time reductions and increased search interactions.
